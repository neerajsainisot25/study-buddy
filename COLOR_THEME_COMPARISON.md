## Accessibility Scores

### Contrast Ratios
- Dark on Light: **11.2:1** (AAA) ✅
- Dark on Teal: **4.8:1** (AA) ✅
- White on Coral: **4.5:1** (AA) ✅
- Teal on Light: **2.3:1** (Large text only) ⚠️

### Color Blindness
- Protanopia: ✅ Distinguishable
- Deuteranopia: ✅ Distinguishable
- Tritanopia: ✅ Distinguishable
- Achromatopsia: ✅ Good contrast

## Design Principles

### 1. Balance
- Cool teal balanced with warm coral

- Calm yet energetic

### 2. Hierarchy
- Teal for primary actions
- Coral for important highlights
- Dark for content
- Light for backgrounds

### 3. Consistency
- Same colors throughout
- Predictable usage
- Clear meaning

### 4. Accessibility
- High contrast ratios
- Color blind friendly
- Clear visual feedback
