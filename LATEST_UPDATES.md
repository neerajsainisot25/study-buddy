# Latest Updates - Version 2.2

## 🎨 New Color Theme: Teal & Coral

### What Changed
The entire application now uses a professional **Teal & Coral** color scheme based on rgb(158, 207, 212).

### New Colors

#### Primary: Teal/Cyan
- **RGB**: rgb(158, 207, 212)
- **HEX**: #9ECFD4
- **Feel**: Calm, professional, trustworthy
- **Use**: Primary buttons, active states, highlights

#### Accent: Coral
- **RGB**: rgb(255, 138, 101)
- **HEX**: #FF8A65
- **Feel**: Warm, friendly, energetic
- **Use**: Secondary buttons, warnings, CTAs

#### Dark: Blue-Gray
- **RGB**: rgb(44, 62, 80)
- **HEX**: #2C3E50
- **Feel**: Sophisticated, stable
- **Use**: Text, dark backgrounds, headers

#### Light: Light Gray
- **RGB**: rgb(236, 240, 241)
- **HEX**: #ECF0F1
- **Feel**: Clean, minimal
- **Use**: Backgrounds, light surfaces

## 📱 Where You'll See It

### Sidebar
- Header: Dark background with teal text
